May 2026 · Data analysis & dashboard

Global health supply chain analysis: SCMS delivery history

Analysed 10,324 global shipment records (2006–2015) from USAID-funded health programs to evaluate transport costs, delivery performance and supply chain efficiency. My part covered data cleaning, DAX queries for the data model, and the dashboard layout and presentation slides. Air freight made up 61% of orders at roughly 3.5 times the cost of road transport, and customs clearance was the leading cause of delays (38%). Recommendations: shift toward road and ocean freight, diversify vendors, and set up regional hubs in Sub-Saharan Africa to lift the 88.51% on-time delivery rate.

Feb 2026 · B2B supply chain orchestrator & traceability platform

GreenLink: ASEAN certified organic farm-to-market supply network

Designed a digitally enabled B2B supply chain orchestrator connecting certified ASEAN organic farmers with urban businesses in Singapore, such as FairPrice and hotels, addressing the region's 30% food waste issue. The concept includes a Farm Management System with IoT sensors for harvest prediction and QR-based ESG traceability, 24–72 hour short-term demand forecasting to counter the bullwhip effect, and a revenue framework built on transaction commissions, ESG reporting subscriptions and certified organic premium margins.

Nov 2025 · Transport & urban planning

Jurong Lake District transport and urban planning proposal

Contributed to a planning proposal for Singapore's Jurong Lake District, which is intended to become the nation's second central business district. Ran a SWOT analysis and mapped transport mismatches with proximity mapping ("Flower of Proximity") and demographic data. Proposed a "20-Minute Town & 45-Minute City" plan with the J-Walk elevated pedestrian network, Underground Bike Links, Vision Zero street design, demand responsive transportation, smart mobility hubs and a climate-resilient cooling network.

Jul 2025 · Business intelligence & data engineering

Global renewable energy analytics and BI dashboard (1965–2022)

Built a single-source-of-truth reporting system for 58 years of renewable energy trends across 251 countries. Consolidated and cleansed 17 raw CSV datasets with Python (pandas), applied master data management, and detected outliers with percentile/IQR methods and an Isolation Forest model. Designed a four-page Power BI dashboard with advanced tooltips, drill through, bookmarks and page navigation, with recommendations on energy diversification and storage investment (for example, 134% growth in solar energy production from 2017 to 2021).
