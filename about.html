About

I hold a Bachelor's degree in International Business Economics from Foreign Trade University in Vietnam, and I am now studying for a Master's in Logistics Engineering & Management at the Technical University of Munich on a DAAD scholarship. Before moving to Munich I worked in textile and garment supply chains in Vietnam. At Madex I coordinated exports, handling documentation, customs procedures and freight bookings. At Crystal Martin Vietnam I worked as an assistant merchandiser, managing SAP data, purchasing and production planning.

Vietnamese is my mother tongue. I work in English (C1, IELTS 7.5), have intermediate German (B2) and am a beginner in Chinese.
